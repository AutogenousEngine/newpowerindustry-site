OUR Story

It all began with hand-sanitizer shortages.

How a distillery the size of a fridge changed the game.

When sanitizer supplies began dwindling in early 2020, a seed was planted in NPI founder and inventor Eli Whipple’s mind. He soon after created his first prototype of a new kind of ethanol distillery; small and affordable enough to allow an individual to profitably distill their own alcohol and hand sanitizer.

He envisioned small-scale producers globally adopting this production equipment (which could also produce liquor, vinegar, and kombucha), effectively solving the numerous challenges with the inherently fragile and extractive centralized manufacturing system, while creating ecologically and economically regenerative revenue streams for everyday people.

What other manufacturing capacity could be distributed?

The idea then spread beyond hand-sanitizer and alcohol to the concept of a resilient “distributed manufacturing”:  manufacturing equipment and capacity to create all the products necessary for a modern resilient community in hyper-local, decentralized, and inherently regenerative ways.

What if industrial manufacturing didn’t have to be bad for our planet? What if economic sovereignty could coincide with vibrant local communities? What if we could reimagine and redesign the industrial manufacturing system to align with the closed loop systems found in nature?

The New Power Industry Venture Studio was born.

The natural next step to make this vision real was to form the New Power Industry Venture Studio to support likeminded regenerative manufacturing startup founders and eco-industrial communities to develop the manufacturing equipment and business models necessary for evolving our society into a future we will be proud to hand down.

Toward the Vitality Economy

New Power Industry is a solutions engineering studio dedicated to the establishment of the Vitality Economy. We spend our days researching and developing appropriate-scale solutions for manufacturing and energy systems that will form the backbone of a truly regenerative, perpetual civilization.

We drive early product development of technologies to support local production of key resources (energy, food, fuel, fiber, water, building materials) and consult on the design and implementation of systems that incorporate these technologies.

From Maximum Extraction...

The way we make, consume, and dispose of the stuff of our modern world is linear, extractive, and highly destructive to our environments and our communities. The technologies and approaches of industry in the world today are mutually reinforcing of this extractive, degenerative relationship to nature.​

... to Value Densification

Establishing a truly lasting, vital society calls for an ecosystem of technologies and techniques for regenerative, closed-loop energy and manufacturing systems at all scales, from the local to the regional to the planetary.

Solutions Engineering in Support of Life

Our design philosophy is one of closing the nature-technology gap, empowering communities to live well adapted to the capacities of their bioregions.

By stepping into our role as a keystone species and regeneratively participating in the ecologies within which we are embedded, we can build a world in which the activities that support our lives support all life.

Toward the Vitality Economy

Vitality Studio forges the building blocks of the Vitality Economy by applying systems design thinking that brings an ecological awareness of complex and highly intelligent natural systems together with advanced engineering know-how and practical execution ability.

We’re assembling a family of technologies to support local production of key resources (energy, food, fuel, fiber, water, building materials) and consulting on the design and implementation of land-based systems that incorporate these technologies.
