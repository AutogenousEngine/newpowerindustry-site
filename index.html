New Power Industry builds your regenerative industrial hardware products with design, engineering, and prototyping services.

We are dedicated to circular design principles where both people and planet thrive.

Our mission is to forge the building blocks of a regenerative, vital society.

We drive early product development of technologies to support local production of key resources (energy, food, fuel, fibre, water, building materials) and consult on the design and implementation of systems that incorporate these technologies.

We specialize in combustion, biomass energy, biochar, gasification, clean cooking, distillation, and carbon capture. We built a bunch of cool things these past few years…
