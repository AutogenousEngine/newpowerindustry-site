How does it work?

“Induction” electric cooking is the most efficient way to cook. The electricity only heats the pot, not the surrounding air or even the cooker. It will not get hot, so you can safely touch it right after cooking, keeping your family safe.

A large battery in the stove allows it to be used when the power is out.
